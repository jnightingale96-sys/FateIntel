EnviroChem Studio v2.22.1 Windows startup hotfix
================================================

1. Extract the complete ZIP to a new normal folder.
2. Do not double-click START_ENVIROCHEM.bat while browsing inside the ZIP.
3. Double-click START_ENVIROCHEM.bat in the extracted folder.
4. Keep the visible console open while EnviroChem is running.

The launcher no longer creates a hidden PowerShell process. Browser readiness
polling uses the local Python environment, and the wrapper remains visible when
startup fails. Diagnostic output is written to STARTUP_LOG.txt.
